WEPWAWET-CORE Live

CPU-to-fabric traffic for one ML-DSA-44 signature, simulated in gem5
Designed by Dr. Mohamed El-Hadedy, Director of RSCL
Simulated, not measured on AUP1. Register blocks are stand-ins that answer after a fixed delay. The two chaining cases describe proposed hardware that is not implemented. Driver sleeps and Python overhead are not simulated.
